### Full description

**Your flight. The actual one.**

Tell us which flight — the flight number or aircraft registration, and the date — and we
turn that flight's real recorded track into a 3D-printed relief poster.

- **Real terrain.** The landscape you flew over, printed as a relief you can run your
  thumb across. Valleys, ridges, the lake you crossed.
- **Colour built in, not painted on.** Green land, blue water, grey where the Alps go
  bare. Each colour is its own filament, printed in place.
- **The path stands at your altitude.** Your flight path is a wall crossing the
  landscape, and its height at every point is the altitude you were flying there. Climb,
  cruise, descent — the whole profile, visible from the side.
- **Your caption, raised in white.** Up to two lines on the black frame: the route on
  top, the registration and date underneath.

Every poster is made for one specific flight. No two are the same, because no two flights
are.

**Good for:** the flight you got your licence on. A first flight in a friend's aircraft.
The scenic route over the Alps. A last flight on a type before it retired.

### Options

**Size**

- **Large — 21.6 × 22.6 cm** — `[PRICE-L]`
- **Medium — 17.0 × 17.9 cm** — `[PRICE-M]`
- **Small — 12.0 × 12.8 cm** — `[PRICE-S]`

Heights are given with a full two-line caption. The caption bar hangs below the frame, so

**Flight path colour** — red, orange, yellow, white, black or pink. Red is the classic;
anything bright reads well against the green and grey. Ask if you want a colour that
isn't listed.

**Caption** — up to two lines, raised in white. Line 1 is the route, line 2 usually the
registration and date. Both are optional; leave them empty for a clean frame.

- **Line 1: max 30 characters** — about 4 words
- **Line 2: max 45 characters** — about 6 words

Those are the lengths that still print at full size. Longer text isn't rejected, it just
gets set smaller to fit. On the small poster keep line 1 to about 20 characters, or the
letters get too fine to read across a room.

## 3 — The order form

Three fields, same on both shops. On Etsy these go in the personalisation box (fields 1
and 2 as free text, colour as a variation if you'd rather it be a dropdown).

### Field 1 — Flight details (free text, required)

**Label:** Your flight details

**Placeholder:**

```
Flight: HB-EZE
Date: 02.08.2026
Route: LSZH → LSZR (Zurich → Altenrhein)
Departure time: 14:20 local
```

**Instructions:**

**Before you order: check your flight is on FlightRadar24.** We print the flight's
recorded track, so the track has to exist. It takes a minute:

1. Go to **flightradar24.com** and search your **flight number** (e.g. `LX1234`) or the
   **aircraft registration** (e.g. `HB-EZE`).
2. Open its **flight history** and find your date.
3. Open that flight and check you can see the **path drawn on the map** — a line from
   departure to arrival, not just a row in a table.

If you can see the line, we can print it. If you can't find it, don't order yet — send us
the details and we'll check it ourselves, free.

Then copy this into the box and fill it in:

```
Flight: (flight number or aircraft registration)
Date: (DD.MM.YYYY)
Route: (departure → arrival airport)
Departure time: (local — only needed if the flight runs several times a day)
```

### Field 2 — Caption (free text, optional)

**Label:** Caption (up to two lines)

**Placeholder:**

```
Line 1: ZURICH → ALTENRHEIN
Line 2: HB-EZE · 02.08.2026
```

**Instructions:** Line 1 is the big one, usually the route. Line 2 is smaller, usually the
registration and date.

- **Line 1: 30 characters max** (about 4 words)
- **Line 2: 45 characters max** (about 6 words)

Spaces count. Longer text isn't rejected — it just gets set smaller to fit the bar.
Capitals, digits, `·`, `-` and `→` all print cleanly. Leave the field empty for a poster
with no caption.

### Field 3 — Flight path colour (dropdown, required)

**Label:** Flight path colour

**Options:** Red (classic) · Orange · Yellow · White · Black · Pink

**Instructions:** This is the colour of the raised path crossing the landscape. Bright
colours stand out most against the green and grey. The frame, caption and terrain colours
are fixed.

### Both shops

**We check before we print.** Every order is verified against FlightRadar24 first. If the
flight isn't there, or the track has gaps that would ruin the poster, we contact you —
you can pick another flight or get a full refund. Nothing gets printed on a guess.

**Flights we can't do**

- **Long-haul** — roughly over 2 hours or 1000 km. Short and regional is what this is for.
- **Older than about 12 months.** Ask anyway; sometimes we can still pull it.
- **Untracked aircraft** — owner-blocked registrations, most military flights, and areas
  with no coverage simply have no track to print.
- **Flights that never left the ground.** No path, nothing to print.
