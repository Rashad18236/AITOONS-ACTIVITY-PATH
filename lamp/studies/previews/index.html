Relief themes and a closer look at the ground

These are flat shaded mock-ups rendered from the real terrain and OpenStreetMap data for three test orders: the Zermatt trail run, the Zürich triathlon and a 5 km city run through the Altstadt. They show what each theme draws and in which colour. The printed relief is a 3D heightmap, so shading and the wall's height will read differently in PLA; the colours and the map content are what to judge here.

Every palette is a starting point to tune against the spools you actually buy. Themes are named by look, never by a race or brand: the marks (M-dot, UTMB, the Boston unicorn, the Strava logo) are trademarks, and the palettes are only inspired by them.

Part 1 · the perspective

Streets, trails and the railway appear when the route is short

Today the relief fetches only main roads (tertiary and up), water and the grey band above 2000 m, whatever the zoom. On a 7 km box that is a satellite view. The proposal adds finer map classes as the ground scale gets closer: village streets below about 35 m per printed mm, signposted hiking routes below 40, railways below 150, buildings as small raised blocks below 12. Zermatt sits at 33 m/mm, so it gains streets, the yellow hiking network and the Gornergrat line; it is still too far out for buildings. The city 5K at 8.5 m/mm gets everything.

The web preview looks from lower down

The three.js preview currently sits high above the poster. The proposal fits the camera to the poster size, at about 35° above the table, with a warm raking key light from the upper left, the way a wall lamp hits it. These two images only sketch the angle change.

Density cap
The existing rule stays: while detail covers more than 15 % of the plate the finest class is dropped first (streets, then trails). Trails mean ways on a signposted hiking route, not every footpath; at Zermatt every footpath turned the plate yellow.
Line width
All detail prints at 1.2 mm (three extrusions). A thinner trail would fall under the 0.84 mm colour floor and merge into the ground.
Buildings
A two-layer roof (0.48 mm) in the detail colour, so the wall stands on the roofs. A 15 m house is 0.4 mm at Zermatt's scale, under the floor, which is why they appear only on boxes under about 2.5 km.

Form ideas for a later round

These need geometry changes, not colours, so they are not in this proposal: a frameless relief that floats on hidden standoffs; a frame with a soft 6 mm radius; a plywood-look frame printed in wood-filled PLA; the caption cut through the frame as a stencil instead of raised; a thin brass-coloured inlay line around the relief opening.

Events considered and where they land

Marathon majors
Boston blue/yellow, New York blue/orange, London, Chicago and Tokyo red: all use the City marathon relief with the route wall in the event colour, chosen per order from the path-colour list.
Obstacle and fitness races
Spartan red/black fits the Triathlon theme; a yellow-on-black race fits Dark mode with a yellow accent.
Open-water swims, winter trail
Alpine winter blues and slate.
Tracker apps
Orange, green or red accents on the dark terrain; one table line each if a customer asks.
Four colours on the relief
Alpine hiking, Alpine winter, Grand tour, Trail and Forest use a fourth AMS slot for streets and trails. The prime tower gets deeper; the plan measures it in Orca before any of these is offered.
